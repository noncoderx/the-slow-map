## GEOGRAPHY OVERVIEW

- **Location:** Trans-Himalayan region, NE Himachal Pradesh. Central coords ~32°14′49″N 78°03′08″E; valley spans ~31°45′–32°20′N, 78°00′–78°30′E.
- **Borders:** Tibet (China) east, Ladakh north, Lahaul west/southwest, Kullu south, Kinnaur southeast.
- **Name meaning:** "the middle land" — between Tibet and rest of India; called "Little Tibet."
- **Elevation range:** valley floor ~2,950–4,100 m (avg ~3,800 m); villages/monasteries from Tabo (~3,050m) to Tashigang (~4,650m) and passes higher.
- **Spiti River:** largest right-bank tributary of the Sutlej, originates from Kunzum Range; Pin River (from Pin Valley) joins at Shishuna; Spiti merges with Sutlej at Khab (Kinnaur). Sutlej itself originates at Lake Rakshastal, Tibet — one of only 3 trans-Himalayan rivers to originate on the Tibetan Plateau.
- **Terrain:** cold high-altitude desert — sparse vegetation, pale/dry soil, bare stratified multicolored mountains; shaped by glacio-fluvial erosion + tectonic uplift over the Quaternary.
- **Climate vs neighbors:** Spiti/Lahaul = cold desert/alpine-glacial (−20 to −30°C winters, short dry summers, semi-arid, Himalayan range blocks monsoon moisture — barley/peas/potatoes only viable crops). Kinnaur (esp. Lower) gets real monsoon rain → temperate forest, unlike Spiti's sparse grasses/shrub/juniper. Vegetation caps ~4,000m; glacier line ~5,000m.
- **Population of Spiti sub-division:** 12,445 (2011 Census); district total 31,564. No verified newer figure exists.
