# Spiti Valley — Trekking, Adventure & High-Value Side-Trips (research pass, Aug 2026)

## 1. Pin-Parvati Pass Trek
| Attribute | Data |
|---|---|
| Max altitude | 5,300-5,321 m (~17,450 ft) |
| Duration | 10-12 days |
| Distance | ~110 km |
| Daily walking | 6-9 hrs typical, up to 10-12 hrs hard days |
| Best season | Mid-July to mid-Sept (narrow — pass must be snow-clear); outer window late June-early Sept |
| Difficulty | "Very difficult" — glacier crossings, moraine, boulder-hopping, unpredictable weather; no technical gear needed but real crevasse/route-finding risk. Experienced high-altitude trekkers only. |
| Base camp | Mud (Mudh) village, Pin Valley, ~3,800m |

Connects lush forested Parvati Valley (Kullu) to barren fossil-strewn Pin Valley (Spiti) — verified real ecological/landscape transition, not exaggeration. Shiva-Parvati symbolic framing used by trek operators (cultural framing, not verified fact). Wildlife en route: blue sheep/bharal, ibex, marmots, occasional snow-leopard signs (tracks/scat) — actual sightings not claimed common.

## 2. Pin Valley National Park
| Attribute | Data |
|---|---|
| Established | 1987 |
| Total area | 1,825 km² (core 675, buffer 1,150) |
| Location | Cold desert, Spiti sub-division |

**Snow leopard numbers — flag inflated claims:** Travel blogs give inconsistent unsourced "12-15" or "about twelve" figures, no scientific citation. Real peer-reviewed data (NCF/PLOS One, PMC8133441, camera-trap Oct2011-Jan2012) measured DENSITY not park headcount: 0.08-0.37 individuals/100km² across ~953km² combined Spiti+Pin Valley+upper Kinnaur study area — didn't isolate Pin-Valley-only figure. Separate 2021 statewide survey (Mongabay) mapped 73 snow leopards across all HP. **Bottom line: genuinely high density/strong reputation (esp. adjacent Kibber Wildlife Sanctuary ~1,000km², est.1992), but any single-digit "X leopards in the park" number in travel blogs = unverified marketing, not science.** Sightings real but rare/unpredictable per every credible source.

Other wildlife (travel-source figures, same caution applies): Siberian ibex (primary breeding ground, rocky slopes, early morning), blue sheep/bharal (more numerous, steep faces) — PMC9302832 independently confirms both as primary snow-leopard prey co-occurring in park.

Best wildlife season: April, May, November, early December (animals move to lower altitude, shoulder months).

Mud (Mudh) village: ~3,800m, last motorable village in Pin Valley, ~30 homes/~200 people, gateway for day hikes.

## 3. Chandratal Lake
| Attribute | Data |
|---|---|
| Altitude | ~4,300m (14,100ft) |
| Location | Lahaul & Spiti district — geographically near Lahaul side of Kunzum Pass, commonly bundled into Spiti circuit trips |
| Name meaning | Sanskrit "chandra"(moon)+"tal"(lake) = "Moon Lake" — literally from crescent shoreline shape (verified real feature) |
| Best season | Mid-June to mid-Sept; Sept often best (clear skies + stable roads + fewer crowds). Access season ~June-early Oct. |

Camping directly on lakeshore BANNED (environmental protection); designated zones 2-3km away, toward Batal side, seasonal tented camps need advance booking.

Permits: no permit for Indian nationals (2026). Foreign nationals need lighter "Beyond Rohtang Pass" permit (₹50) — NOT the full ILP/PAP needed for Kaza/Tabo/Dhankar circuit.

## 4. Kunzum Pass
| Attribute | Data |
|---|---|
| Altitude | 4,551m (14,931ft, Wikipedia) — other sources round to 4,590m |
| Road | NH505; 122km from Manali, 79km from Kaza |
| Season | June-October |
| Foreigner permit | None required for Kaza-Losar-Kunzum-Gramphoo-Manali route |

Kunzum Mata (Kunzum Devi) temple at the pass — worshipped by Hindus and Buddhists, prayer flags/bells, adjacent Buddhist chorten. Local custom: circle temple for safe passage before crossing. Literal/symbolic gateway between Lahaul and Spiti.

## 5. Other Treks / Cross-Region Routes

**Pin-Bhaba Pass (Kinnaur↔Spiti):** Kafnu village (Kinnaur, ~7,878ft) → Pin Bhaba Pass (~16,100-16,125ft) → Mud village. ~50-60km, 7-8 days. "Moderately difficult" — easier than Pin-Parvati, altitude is main driver not technical terrain. Same lush-to-desert transition motif (Bhaba Valley→Pin Valley).

**Parang La (Spiti↔Ladakh):** Kibber/Dumla (near Kaza) → Parang La (5,538-5,580m) → Pare Chu valley → Korzok village, Tso Moriri shore (Ladakh). 100+km, ~8 days. "Challenging to difficult" — remote, no habitation/rescue en route, consecutive days above 4,200-5,500m; prior high-altitude experience recommended. Best season mid-July-early Sept. Follows ancient Spiti-Ladakh/Tibet trade route. Primary/most-cited real Spiti-Ladakh trekking connection. (One source mentions Takling La as a rarely-used alternative, largely superseded — single-source, low confidence.)

## 6. Photography — Named Viewpoints & Timing

| Location | Cited detail | Best timing |
|---|---|---|
| Key Monastery | Classic postcard shot from opposite riverbank/ridge, not from within complex | Dawn/sunrise |
| Dhankar Monastery/Lake | Monastery (12,700ft) overlooks Spiti-Pin confluence — that viewpoint itself named photo spot; Dhankar Lake separately cited for night-sky/stargazing | Confluence good most of day; lake mainly for stars |
| Langza | Giant seated Buddha as foreground/silhouette for Milky Way shots; fossil-hunting village (~200M-year-old marine fossils visible on slopes) | Night — Milky Way |
| Komic | Highest motorable villages claim (4,587m/15,050ft) — exceptionally clear/dark skies, star trails | Night |
| Hikkim | Grouped with Komic/Langza in standard ~44km day-loop from Kaza; world's highest post office (since 1983) — cultural stop not landscape viewpoint | — |

**Dhankar Lake trek specifics (IndiaHikes + independent sources):** trailhead near Dhankar new monastery (village 3,870m); ~3km one-way, ~280m ascent; 45min-2.5hrs one-way (wide range across sources, treat as 1-2.5hr realistic); lake altitude 4,270m (14,010ft); moderate, steep non-technical, altitude/breathlessness the main challenge.

General best photography season: May-Oct for green/vibrant landscapes + open roads; winter (Dec-Feb) for dramatic snow but access far more restricted — no hard data on how many operators run winter trips, treat as logistically harder not simply "also good."

## 7. Permits — cross-cutting note
Indian nationals: none needed anywhere in standard circuit or Chandratal. Foreign nationals: PAP required for interior circuit (2+ group at Shimla office; solo OK at Reckong Peo/Pooh/Kaza). e-Aagman QR pass also required, NOT a substitute for PAP — carrying only one reportedly gets foreigners turned away from monasteries/villages. Chandratal only needs lighter "Beyond Rohtang" ₹50 permit.
