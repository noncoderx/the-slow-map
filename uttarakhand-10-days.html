§ 01 — Why this combo

Ridge, then water. In that order.

Landour and Naukuchiatal aren't accidentally paired. They bracket the two halves of Uttarakhand — Garhwal's colonial-ridge quiet to the west, Kumaon's lake-and-orchard quiet to the east — and the journey between them passes through Rishikesh, which is the perfect mid-point to let the hills fall off your shoulders and the plains tire before you climb again.

Why Landour first

You arrive tired from Delhi. Landour hands you tea, a fireplace, a bookshelf, and a walk — in that order. The altitude is modest, the pace is built for recovery, and by Day 4 you'll feel rested enough to do a proper drive across the state without it breaking you.

If you go the other way round (Naukuchiatal first, Landour last), you arrive in Delhi too tired to enjoy the final leg. This is the order our own editors prefer.

Why Naukuchiatal second

After four days of reading and walking, you're ready to sit by water and do nothing. Naukuchiatal is built for that. It rewards stillness. Your mornings become kayak mornings, your afternoons become hammock afternoons, and you return to Delhi the emptiest-headed you've been in years.

The nine-cornered lake is also the one place in this trip where children can run unsupervised for hours. End the trip with that gift.

§ 02 — Day by day

Ten days. Four of them still.

A 4-4 split with a transition day in the middle. Arrive Delhi → Dehradun → Landour. Cross to Naukuchiatal on Day 5. Return via Kathgodam on Day 10.

Day 01 · Arrival

Delhi → Dehradun → Landour Landour

Board Shatabdi 12017 at 06:50 AM from New Delhi. Reach Dehradun by 12:40 PM. Pre-booked taxi to Landour — 35 km, ~1h 30m. Check into Rokeby Manor or La Villa Bethany. Walk to Char Dukan for first chai. Dinner at Emily's. Sleep early.

Day 02 · The ridge walk

Lal Tibba at dawn Landour

6:30 AM start. 3 km up through deodar past Ruskin Bond's Ivy Cottage. Himalayan panorama at the summit if the weather is clear. Breakfast at Anil's Café — pancakes + ginger-lemon-honey tea. Long afternoon at Landour Bakehouse for rose-and-almond cake.

Day 03 · Sister's Bazaar

Colonial Landour Landour

Walk to Sister's Bazaar — stop at Prakash's Store for peanut butter, apricot jam, conversation. Long lunch at Café Ivy. Evening: the British cemetery walk + St. Paul's Church at golden hour. Dinner in. Read.

Day 04 · Day trip

Dhanaulti + Tiger Falls Landour

Choice of one. Dhanaulti (28 km, 1h 10m) for pines + eco-park, or Tiger Falls near Chakrata (75 km, 2h 30m) for India's tallest waterfall in Uttarakhand (312 ft). If with children, pick Dhanaulti.

Day 05 · The Bridge

Landour → Rishikesh → Naukuchiatal Transition

The long driving day. Leave Landour by 7 AM. Reach Rishikesh by 10 AM (breakfast at Beatles Café or Little Buddha). Continue east — Haridwar bypass → Najibabad → Ramnagar → Haldwani → Kathgodam → up to Naukuchiatal. ~350 km, 8–9 hours with stops. Check into Naukuchia House or Cottages@Village by dusk. Keep Day 5 light — you'll be tired.

Day 06 · Slow start

The nine-cornered lake Naukuchiatal

Sleep in after yesterday's drive. Breakfast on the deck. Walk the Eight Corners Trail (2 km, flat). Lunch at Café Magic Forest. Evening shikara ride at sunset. Spot the nine corners (locals say it earns you moksha).

Day 07 · Bhimtal & books

iHeart Café afternoon Naukuchiatal

Morning walk to Jangalia Gaon (3.5 km). Lunch at Aipan Restaurant — the Kumaoni thali (Bhatt, Aloo Gutke, Nimbu Saan). Afternoon at iHeart Café in Bhimtal with two new books from their shelf. Butterfly Research Centre at 4 PM (entry ₹40, colonial bungalow).

Day 08 · Mukteshwar day trip

Ridge + waterfall Naukuchiatal

Drive 47 km to Mukteshwar (1h 30m). Lunch at Café De Mukteshwar (Tripadvisor #2 of 17). The Bhalu Gaad waterfall trek (park at Dhari, 2 km each way, three stream crossings). Stop at Kilmora's women-cooperative café on the way back — buy apricot oil and jam.

Day 09 · Sattal birding

Reserve & kayak Naukuchiatal

7 AM: guided birding walk at Sattal reserve (₹1,500–2,500 via Sattal Birding or Wild Kumaon). 500+ bird species; April–May is breeding-plumage season. 11:30 AM: 30-min kayak on the lake. Evening at The Birdsong Café in Mukteshwar for a final long tea.

Day 10 · Home

Naukuchiatal → Delhi Naukuchiatal

Slow morning. Pack. Breakfast. Leave by noon. Taxi to Kathgodam (26 km, 1h). Shatabdi 12039 from Kathgodam at 15:35 → New Delhi 22:40. You'll be emptier-headed and fuller-hearted than when you left.

§ 03 — The Bridge

How to cross the state on Day 5.

The hardest logistical part of this trip. Landour to Naukuchiatal = ~350 km across the Kumaon–Garhwal line. Three options, ranked by what we'd actually do.

Recommended

The One-Day Drive

8–9 hours · ₹9,000–14,000
  • Leave Landour 7 AM · breakfast stop at Rishikesh
  • Haridwar bypass · lunch at Najibabad (Irani café)
  • Ramnagar → Haldwani → Kathgodam · tea
  • Up to Naukuchiatal · arrive 5–6 PM
  • Best if your taxi driver is experienced & calm
With children

Split the Drive

2 days · Rishikesh overnight
  • Day 5: Landour → Rishikesh (4h) · stay overnight
  • Cafés by the Ganges, Beatles Ashram walk
  • Day 6: Rishikesh → Naukuchiatal (5h)
  • Adds one night, removes the exhaustion
  • If kids younger than 8, this is the better pick
Fastest

Fly via Delhi

~6 hours door-to-door · ₹10,000–18,000
  • Landour → Jolly Grant (1.5h) · flight to Delhi (50m)
  • Delhi → Pantnagar flight (40m) · taxi to Naukuchiatal (1.5h)
  • Feels faster but four hand-offs + two airports
  • Only worth it if Day 5 is critical
  • Honest opinion: the one-day drive is nicer
