§ 4 — The rules that keep it fun

Six rules. Break them and it dies.

Every publication that lost its soul broke one of these. Every one that kept growing slowly did not.

i.
Keep your day job till Year 3.

Financial pressure is the #1 killer of editorial quality. Runway is your secret weapon.

ii.
One issue a month. Not more, not less.

More and quality dies. Less and readers forget you. One per month for 60 months = a real body of work.

iii.
Never take outside money.

No VC, no angel, not even friends. Outside cash demands growth that kills the soul.

iv.
If you stop enjoying it, pause.

Don't grind. Rested writing is 10× better than tired writing. Readers can feel the difference.

v.
Hand-verify everything.

The whole thesis is trust. One fabricated detail on one issue undoes five years of slow work.

vi.
Don't hire until Year 4.

The day you hire, you become a manager. Make as long as you can before you manage.
